# Hackathon #2 Performance Reflection

Participating in Hackathon #2 provided an invaluable opportunity to translate theoretical data pipelines into a functional operational model for Kenya Pipeline Company (KPC) infrastructure. 

Our team structured workflow efficiently by delegating responsibilities based on core competencies: data engineering and pipeline setup were handled by managing ingestion and structured cleaning; modeling and machine learning leads managed feature engineering and algorithm tuning; while dashboarding and executive storytelling leads focused on translating analytical outputs into clear business value. 

Our biggest analytical hurdle centered on aligning synthetic sensor degradation signatures with realistic operational constraints and handling missing variance across multi-node telemetry feeds. We overcame this by implementing robust feature scaling and validating our residual calculations against known baseline parameters. 

To ensure our final recommendations remained strictly actionable for senior leadership, we avoided overly complex technical jargon, framing every algorithmic improvement around tangible business metrics—specifically reducing emergency repair expenditures, minimizing throughput-stoppage durations, and optimizing deployment schedules. This ensured the deliverables bridged the gap between advanced data science and practical executive decision-making.
